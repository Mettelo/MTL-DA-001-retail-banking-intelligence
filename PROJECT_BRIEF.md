# Project Brief

## Project ID

MTL-DA-001

## Working Title

Retail Banking Customer & Portfolio Intelligence

## Engagement Type

Simulated commercial analytics engagement delivered through Mettelo Project Studio.

## Background

A mid-sized retail banking organisation has grown its customer base and product portfolio over several years, but reporting has evolved independently across customer operations, lending, finance and commercial teams.

Senior stakeholders receive multiple reports that do not always agree. Measures such as active customer, account value, repayment performance, customer value and portfolio risk are calculated differently across teams. Existing reporting is heavily spreadsheet-based and provides limited visibility of how customer behaviour, transaction activity and lending performance relate to one another.

Leadership wants a more reliable analytical view of the business before making decisions about customer retention, portfolio management, service operations and future investment.

## Business Problem

The organisation does not currently have a consistent analytical view of customers, accounts, transactions and lending activity.

The project team has been commissioned to investigate the available data and develop an evidence-based analytical solution that enables decision-makers to understand:

- customer and account activity;
- transactional behaviour;
- lending and repayment performance;
- customer concentration and segmentation;
- portfolio and operational trends;
- potential areas requiring further investigation.

The team is not expected to simply reproduce a predetermined dashboard. It must determine the appropriate analytical approach, define defensible metrics, document assumptions and communicate findings to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

## Project Objectives

By the end of the engagement, the team should have:

1. assessed the quality and usability of the supplied source data;
2. documented key entities, relationships and business rules;
3. developed a reproducible analytical dataset or model;
4. agreed and documented appropriate business metrics;
5. investigated material customer, transaction and portfolio patterns;
6. created decision-support outputs suitable for senior stakeholders;
7. documented limitations, risks and recommended next steps;
8. presented the final solution and findings to a Mettelo review panel.

## Scope

The detailed analytical scope should be refined during discovery. The team should avoid assuming that every available field must appear in the final solution.

## Out of Scope

Unless subsequently approved through a formal project change:

- production deployment into a live banking environment;
- decisions about individual real customers;
- automated credit decisions;
- use of personally identifiable information;
- representation that the simulated organisation is a real Mettelo client.

## Delivery Expectations

This project should be treated as a team delivery engagement. Participants are expected to use structured task management, version control, peer review and documented decision-making throughout the project.

The final solution must be reproducible and understandable by another analyst who was not part of the original team.
